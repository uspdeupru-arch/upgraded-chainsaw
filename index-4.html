01 / WHO WE ARE

A union of independent voices.

SCHTAHLLAND brings countries together without erasing their independence. Cooperation is the foundation; sovereignty remains with each member.

02 / CORE PRINCIPLES

Cooperation with purpose.

The alliance is designed to coordinate support while leaving room for each country to act independently.

Mutual support

When a member faces a threat or attack, the alliance may coordinate diplomatic and political backing, economic and resource assistance, defensive support, and humanitarian relief.

Diplomatic relations

The union can maintain relations with non-members and conclude cooperation, friendship and non-aggression agreements. Member countries may also pursue their own external agreements.

03 / INSTITUTIONS

A defined leadership structure.

Leadership and senior officials coordinate union-wide decisions. Members participate in public and internal matters.

01 / EXECUTIVE

Union leadership

The leader directs the union. The right hand serves as deputy and successor when the leader is absent or unable to serve. The left hand oversees the union's military command.

02 / SENIOR COUNCIL

Senior officials

Senior officials include the leader, deputies, military commanders, long-standing members known as Survivors, and honorary members. Each has one vote.

03 / LEGISLATIVE

Union parliament

Representatives are appointed by the leader for two-year terms. Parliament proposes and approves internal laws; a two-thirds vote can override a leadership veto.

Member participation

Ordinary members vote on public decisions, internal laws and member-related matters.

Senior decisions

Important matters are decided by the leader or by a senior-official vote, depending on the subject.

Transparency

Senior meetings and decisions are visible to members; major documents are kept in a digital archive.

04 / ECONOMY

A shared currency framework.

The union's common financial framework exists alongside the independent economic policies of its members.

ℛℳ

Reichsmark

The Reichsmark is the union's common currency. A central bank manages the union budget and is accountable to senior officials on important decisions. Funding may come from annual member contributions, union income and voluntary contributions.

05 / HEADQUARTERS

Proviz Palace.

The permanent seat of the union's leadership and its central diplomatic work.

The heart of SCHTAHLLAND

Proviz Palace houses the offices of the leader and deputies, the senior officials' meeting hall, diplomatic reception facilities and the union media centre. It is also the official residence of the leader and remains open around the clock. Visits are arranged in advance.
